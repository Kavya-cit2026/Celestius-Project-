    About

    One Campus. Countless Ways to Belong.

    CIT ClubSphere is a centralized discovery platform concept designed to bring the clubs and event experiences of Chennai Institute of Technology together. Whether you enjoy coding, AI, design, theatre, dance, music, writing, or interdisciplinary innovation, the platform helps you discover communities that match your interests.

    Independent student project. ClubSphere is a prototype and is not an official Chennai Institute of Technology platform. It is not endorsed by the college or by any club listed here.
